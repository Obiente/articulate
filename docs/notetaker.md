# Notetaker

Open **Notetaker** in the sidebar to find conversations and personal notes saved on this device.

## Speak your thoughts

Choose **Speak your thoughts** to record from your microphone. Articulate keeps
the original transcript and turns committed speech into an editable notes
document using the downloaded local notes model. Spoken notes can update after
15 seconds and 12 new words; finishing requests a final update immediately.
The transcript shows speech sooner while the model prepares the document.

Press **Ctrl+Alt+N** anywhere to start a quick note, and press it again to finish.
Change this shortcut in Settings. Quick-note capture stays inside Articulate and
does not type into the foreground application. It cannot start during another
dictation or call recording.

After a spoken note finishes, the local model files it into a matching topic or
creates a new topic. An uncertain match becomes a separate inbox note. Each
recording stays saved independently, with its original transcript. Topic notes
are generated from the recordings together, rather than concatenating their
individual summaries. Ordinary dictations are not automatically filed.

Open a topic's **Dictations** tab to read or move a recording. Moving changes its
membership and refreshes both affected topic documents. Other recordings and
handwritten paragraphs stay in place. You can move it back, choose a different
topic, or keep it separately. A populated topic must have its recordings moved
before it can be deleted. If the model is unavailable, the recording remains
saved separately and can be filed later.

## Write a personal note

Choose **New note** and start typing in **Notes**. You do not need a microphone, a speech model, or a recording. The title is editable. Changes save after a short pause, when you leave the note, and when Articulate closes.

The save status appears beside the back button. If saving fails, keep the note open and choose **Retry saving**, or copy the text. Returning to the hub keeps the same note; it does not create another one.

## Record and write together

Choose **Record a conversation** to open audio setup and capture controls. During a capture, **Transcript** shows committed speech and the revisable live tail. **Notes** is one editable document for important details, decisions and next steps. With the optional local notes model installed, it updates during the call while you write. Expand **Transcript sources** to check the supporting excerpts.

Automatic call updates use committed speech, wait at least 45 seconds between attempts, and need at least 40 new words while recording. After the call finishes, remaining speech can trigger an immediate final update. Processing time depends on your computer. Use **Live updates** to pause or resume, or **Update notes** to request an update yourself. Manually typed paragraphs are retained; review generated wording before relying on it.

Conversation titles are generated by the same local model as notes, using topics across the conversation. New calls display **Conversation** until a title is ready. Titles can evolve with later notes updates; editing a title yourself keeps your chosen name on subsequent updates. If the notes model is unavailable, no title is invented from the first sentence.

You can return to Notetaker while capture continues. The active conversation card's **Open current note** action reopens that capture without starting or stopping recording. Audio setup and the finish control remain available inside the conversation workspace.

## Find and review past notes

The hub lists conversations and personal notes with their title, local date/time, and a text preview. Conversations also show their captured duration. Search matches titles and previews; filters show conversations or personal notes separately.

Open a conversation to edit **Notes** or read and search its **Transcript**. Each conversation keeps its own document and generation status. **Update notes** uses the optional separately downloaded local model. Sources remain available in an expandable section, with timestamps referring to speaker turns. The former Highlights and Assort meeting-suggestion panels are no longer part of the workspace.

## Copy, export, and delete

**Copy notes** copies the current document. Personal notes also offer **Export notes**. **More → Copy full note** and **Export full note** include the document and transcript. Older saved excerpts remain in full exports for compatibility; a legacy summary can appear when there is no notes document. Review the complete export before sharing it.

Transcript exports remain available as plain text, Markdown, SRT, and WebVTT. **More → Delete note** asks for confirmation and removes the saved item from this device.

Notes use the same local history storage as transcripts. For an older saved summary without a document, **Restore generated notes** brings its text into the editor. Nothing is sent to a cloud notes service, and this workspace does not connect to a calendar or save audio.

Use **Search** in the sidebar to find words or phrases throughout saved transcripts and personal notes, beyond the hub's title and preview search. Results open the original session.
